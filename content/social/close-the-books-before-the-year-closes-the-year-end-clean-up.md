## LinkedIn

A $14,000 piece of equipment sitting in "Supplies" is a quiet October problem and a loud January one.

Most Port Arthur physicians we meet are not behind on their taxes. They are behind on their books. The operating account has not been reconciled since August, and one credit card holds lab supplies next to a weekend in Galveston.

Here is the math. A solo practice with about 1,200 transactions and 5 percent miscoded has 60 entries to find and fix. At an assumed $200 an hour, five hours of cleanup adds $1,000 to the return before anyone looks for a deduction.

The fix is a Q4 clean-up. Reconcile every bank, card, payer, and processor account, separate personal spending from practice spending, and correct miscategorized transactions before December 31.
