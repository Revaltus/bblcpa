A pipefitter works 12-hour days for six weeks of turnaround and earns $9,216 in overtime. How much can he deduct? $3,072.

The new federal overtime deduction covers only the premium, the extra half in time and a half. Social Security and Medicare tax still apply to every dollar. In the 22 percent bracket, the deduction saves about $676.

If you run a dental office with hourly hygienists or a vet clinic paying techs overtime, your payroll records decide what your employees can claim. Regular wages, FLSA overtime, and double-time extras need separate pay codes.
