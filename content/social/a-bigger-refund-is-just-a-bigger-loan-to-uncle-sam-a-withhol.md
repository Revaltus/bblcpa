## LinkedIn

A $3,120 refund is $120 out of every biweekly paycheck that went to the IRS instead of your household.

Over-withholding evenly across the year means the government holds an average of about $1,560 of your money. Carry a card balance at 22 percent APR and that idle cash costs you roughly $340 a year in interest. Park it in savings at 4 percent and you would have earned about $62.

The 2025 tax law changes make this a live question. The standard deduction, child tax credit, SALT cap, and new tips, overtime, and senior deductions all shift your real tax bill, but most 2025 paychecks were withheld under the old rules.

The fix is a three-step checkup: compare last year's return to your pay stubs, run the IRS Tax Withholding Estimator, then update your W-4, W-4R, or quarterly estimates. A dentist buying equipment in October and a retiree taking IRA distributions need different answers, and year-round tax support keeps that number current when life changes.
