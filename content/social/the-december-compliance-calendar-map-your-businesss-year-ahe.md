## LinkedIn

Say a three-physician group leases 4,200 square feet with a 4 percent annual escalator and a 120-day notice requirement. Miss that window and the lease renews on its own, along with your chance to ask for a lower escalator or a tenant improvement allowance.

That quiet kind of deadline is why we wrote a December compliance calendar guide for Port Arthur physicians. The idea is one list of every insurance renewal, lease notice window, license date, payroll filing, and Texas franchise tax deadline. Add a decision date 60 to 120 days before each renewal, and give every entry an owner so nothing lapses or auto-renews unnoticed.

December works because patient schedules thin out and the January and May filings are right behind. A few focused hours now means you decide on your timeline, not on somebody else's notice.
